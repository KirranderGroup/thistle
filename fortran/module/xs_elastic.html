elastic_pairs

PyXSCAT - rotationally averaged elastic scattering by density-weighted pairs

 I(q) = sum_{mu nu la si} A_mu,nu B_la,si < chi_mu chi_nu | K_l(q r12) | chi_la chi_si >

with K_0 = j0 (isotropic) or K_2 = j2 P2 (second Legendre moment about z). A = B = D gives the elastic scattering <|F(q)|^2>; A /= B the coherent mixed (transition) terms (symmetrised).

The pair density of the elastic term is a product, D (x) D, so the density can be folded into each primitive-shell pair BEFORE the pairs are combined: every pair becomes one Hermite charge distribution W(tuv) at its centre P,

 rho_P(r) = (pi/p)^{3/2} K_ab sum_tuv W(tuv) Lambda_tuv(r - P),

and the elastic term is a sum over pairs of pairs,

 I(q) = sum_{P,Q} pref_P pref_Q exp(-q^2/4p - q^2/4p') sum_m S_m(P - Q) f_m(q, |P - Q|),
 S_m  = sum_{TUV} U(TUV) sum_{ijk: T+U+V-i-j-k = m} a(T,i) a(U,j) a(V,k) hx^(T-2i) hy^(U-2j) hz^(V-2k),
 U(TUV) = sum_{tuv + t'u'v' = TUV} W_P(tuv) W_Q(t'u'v') (-1)^(t'+u'+v'),
 f_m  = (-q^2)^m j_m(qh)/(qh)^m.

Compared with the quartet kernel (which must loop over every Cartesian component of the four functions because a general 2RDM does not factorise), the work per pair of pairs is one convolution of two Hermite tables and one radial sum. The radial functions j_m(x)/x^m are evaluated stably at every x, so coincident and nearly coincident pair centres need no special treatment and lose no digits.

Subroutines

public subroutine elastic_pairs(nprim, nao, nq, x, y, z, ga, l, m, n, group, contr, coeff, q, da, db, lorder, screen, res, npairs_out, ierr)

I(q) at every q for the one-particle matrices da, db (elastic for da = db, coherent mixed otherwise), K_0 for lorder = 0 and K_2 for lorder = 2, by the pair-of-pairs algorithm of the module description. Pairs whose bound is below screen are dropped; npairs_out returns the number of significant pairs and of pairs of pairs evaluated; ierr = 0 on success.
