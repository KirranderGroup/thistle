PyXSCAT - Fourier transforms of basis-function products at fixed q

 F(q, mu, nu) = int chi_mu(r) chi_nu(r) exp(+i q.r) dr

for an arbitrary list of scattering vectors (no orientational average). Everything that depends on the molecular orientation (aligned or oriented molecules, detector images) follows from these by contraction with density matrices, because the plane wave factorises:

 elastic      F(q)  = sum D_mu,nu F_mu,nu(q)
 two-electron I2(q) = sum Gamma_{mu nu, la si} F_mu,nu(q) F_la,si(q)^*

Each primitive pair is expanded in Hermite Gaussians at the pair centre P; the transform of a Hermite Gaussian is analytic:

 int Lambda_tuv(r-P) exp(iq.r) = (pi/p)^{3/2} exp(-q^2/4p) exp(iq.P)
                                 (i qx)^t (i qy)^u (i qz)^v

so there is no Bessel expansion and no special treatment of coincident centres.

public subroutine pair_transform(nprim, nao, nq, x, y, z, ga, l, m, n, group, contr, coeff, qv, screen, f, ierr)

F(q, mu, nu) for every scattering vector qv(:, iq) and every pair of contracted functions, into the complex array f(nq, nao, nao). Primitive-shell pairs whose Gaussian prefactor (times the largest coefficients) is below screen are skipped; ierr = 0 on success.
