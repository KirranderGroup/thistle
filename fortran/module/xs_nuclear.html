PyXSCAT - electron-nucleus term of electron scattering

In the first Born approximation a fast electron is scattered by the full charge distribution, nuclei and electrons:

 dsigma/dOmega = 4 / (a0^2 q^4) < | sum_A Z_A exp(iq.R_A) - F(q) |^2 >  + inelastic

which, rotationally averaged, needs one quantity beyond X-ray scattering, the electron-nucleus cross term

 I_ne(q) = sum_A Z_A sum_mu,nu D_mu,nu int chi_mu chi_nu K_l(q, r - R_A) dr

with K_0 = j0(q|h|) (isotropic signal) or K_2 = j2(q|h|) P2(cos theta_h) (second Legendre moment about z, aligned molecules).

Each primitive pair is expanded in Hermite Gaussians at the pair centre P:

 int Lambda_tuv(r - P) j0(q|r - A|) dr = (pi/p)^{3/2} exp(-q^2/4p) d^{t+u+v}/dh^tuv j0(q|h|),
 h = P - A,

and the Cartesian derivatives of the radial function j0(q|h|) follow from the McMurchie-Davidson recursion with the auxiliary functions

 f_n(h) = (1/h d/dh)^n j0(q h) = (-q^2)^n j_n(qh) / (qh)^n.

j2 P2 follows from Hobson's theorem: j2(qh) P2(cos theta) = [3 d^2/dhz^2 + q^2] j0(qh) / (2 q^2). Nothing is singular when a nucleus sits on a pair centre (h = 0).
