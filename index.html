THISTLE¶

Ab initio X-ray and electron scattering from electronic wavefunctions.

THISTLE computes what gas-phase X-ray scattering and electron diffraction experiments measure, directly from the wavefunction of a quantum chemistry calculation, without the independent atom model. It takes a Gaussian basis and the one- and two-particle density matrices of any method (Hartree–Fock, CASSCF, CI, coupled cluster) and returns rotationally averaged, partially aligned and fixed-orientation scattering for X-rays and electrons, with the independent atom model as a reference.

The code has three layers:

  • thistle, the simple interface: load an output file, get a Molecule, ask it for scattering, plot and save the result; input files, the thistle command and Slurm submission.

  • pyxscat, the kernel-level API: basis sets, density matrices, every scattering kind, the adapters for PySCF, Molden, OpenMolcas and Molpro, CI and CCSD densities.

  • The Fortran kernels (src/fortran), compiled into libpyxscat and called through a C interface: the analytic integrals, screened and parallel (OpenMP).
